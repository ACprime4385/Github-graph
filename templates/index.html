What is DevGraph?

DevGraph maps the hidden social graph between GitHub developers using a graph database. Search any GitHub username to discover their direct followers, second-degree connections (followers of followers), and language-based networks.

Why a Graph Database?

Traditional SQL databases require expensive JOIN operations for multi-hop queries. Neo4j traverses relationships natively, making 2-hop and 3-hop queries fast and natural. Social networks are graphs -- modeling them as graphs is the right choice.

Features

  • 1-hop: Direct followers ranked by follower count
    • \
  • 2-hop: Followers of followers with mutual connection count
  • Language Network: Developers who share programming languages
  • Auto-loading: Searches automatically build network data
  • Mutual Members: See exactly who connects you to 2nd-degree contacts
